# Lake Superior winter scene

The owner's two winter photographs locate the start on the grassy slope above the beach area in Bethel. The camera locations are about eight metres apart. The virtual start uses the second photograph's location and faces 249 degrees, toward the beach between the two recorded camera headings. Camera height drops to a seated rider's perspective.

The on-disk grid stores centimetres above the lake surface. The hill and the ride both use the sampled 1-metre USGS bare-earth DEM. The local mesh samples at two metres around the run and six to eight metres farther out. Lake level in the DEM is 385.22 metres NAVD88. The start is 9.29 metres above it, the near shore is roughly 85 metres down the centre sightline, and the opposite bank begins to rise around 260 metres out. The ride finishes on shore. The reference frame is NAD83 UTM zone 18N. Its origin is E 510779.77, N 4612147.89. Forward follows the 249-degree grid bearing and right follows 339 degrees. A spring orthophoto supplies the principal roof outline, about 26 by 13 metres. Sampled laser returns over the building put the upper roof about 4.1 metres above ground. The winter photos guide the gable, dark siding, short rail, tracked snow and leafless woodland. Roof details, rail dimensions and background snow marks are estimates. Each new pair of sled tracks follows the rider’s route. [New York State 2021 orthophotography](https://orthos.its.ny.gov/arcgis/rest/services/wms/2021/MapServer)

Tree groups follow local high points in the USGS 2018 Central New York lidar canopy, sampled to a two-metre grid. The tree shapes represent winter crowns. The simulator's snow, lighting and ice represent the photographed winter conditions.
