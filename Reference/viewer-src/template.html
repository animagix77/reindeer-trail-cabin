Water notes

From the well to the tap

Walk up the wooden steps from the driveway. Beside the upper railing post on your left, a blue cap marks the cabin’s well. A submerged pump draws water up to the house. Down in the basement, two pressure tanks keep water flowing between pump cycles.

The January 2024 inspection also recorded a separate 40-gallon tank for hot water.
