The cabin

A family place
by Binh & Rose

Come for the woods.
Stay a little longer.

Morning coffee tastes better on the covered deck. Head out for a walk in the Smallwood Forest Reserve, catch a show at nearby Bethel Woods, or spend the afternoon right here. When the stars come out, there’s a circle of Adirondack chairs waiting by the fire.

Room to settle in

A queen bedroom, a double room and a bunk room sleep the family. Upstairs, the open loft has a queen sleeper and a view over the vaulted living room.

Life at the cabin

Cook in the full kitchen or grill on the deck. Wi-Fi and driveway parking are included. Steps lead to the entrance on our sloping lot.

Around Smallwood

The Forest Reserve is about a five-minute walk away. Bethel Woods Center for the Arts is about ten minutes away by car.
